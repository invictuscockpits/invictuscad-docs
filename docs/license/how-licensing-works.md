# How Licensing Works

InvictusCAD is a **one-time purchase**. There's no subscription.

- **Your license never expires.** It runs every version of InvictusCAD released while your updates
  are current, for as long as you like.
- **Updates for 12 months.** Each license includes the updates released in the 12 months after you
  buy it. Your **updates-until date** is in your license email, on
  [invictuscad.com/license](https://invictuscad.com/license), and under **Help > License**.
- **After that,** keep using the versions you have, or [renew](renew.md) for another 12 months.
  If you install a version released after your updates ended, InvictusCAD tells you and you can
  reinstall an earlier one.
- **Two computers.** A license can be active on 2 computers at a time, such as a shop PC and a
  laptop. [Move it](move-computers.md) whenever you like.
- **Refunds** within 14 days of purchase; see the
  [refund policy](https://invictuscad.com/legal/refunds).

Paddle, our reseller, handles payment, sales tax and VAT, and invoices.
