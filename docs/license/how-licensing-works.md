# How Licensing Works

Cahaba Studio is a **one-time purchase**. There's no subscription. Prices are on
[cahaba3d.com/pricing](https://cahaba3d.com/pricing).

| | License | Lifetime license |
|---|---|---|
| Expires | Never | Never |
| Updates included | 12 months; [renew](renew.md) for more | Every update, for as long as Cahaba Studio is developed |
| Computers | 2 at a time | 2 at a time |

- **Your license never expires.** It runs every version of Cahaba Studio released while your updates
  are current, for as long as you like.
- **Your updates date** is in your license email, on
  [cahaba3d.com/license](https://cahaba3d.com/license), and under **☰ › Help › License**.
- **After that,** keep using the versions you have, or [renew](renew.md) for another 12 months.
  If you install a version released after your updates ended, it opens
  [read-only](read-only.md) and tells you; renew, or reinstall an earlier version.
- **Two computers.** A license can be active on 2 computers at a time, such as a shop PC and a
  laptop. [Move it](move-computers.md) whenever you like.
- **Works offline.** Cahaba Studio needs the internet only to start the trial and to activate. When
  it's online it checks in once a day in the background.
- **Refunds** within 14 days of purchase; see the
  [refund policy](https://cahaba3d.com/legal/refunds).

Paddle, our reseller, handles payment, sales tax and VAT, and invoices.

## What Cahaba Studio sends

To activate and check in, Cahaba Studio sends your license key, an anonymous identifier for the
computer (a one-way hash, not your hardware's serial numbers) and the computer's name, so you can
tell your computers apart on the website. Nothing about your designs is ever sent. See the
[privacy policy](https://cahaba3d.com/legal/privacy).
