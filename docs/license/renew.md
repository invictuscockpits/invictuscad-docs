# Renew Updates

A renewal adds 12 months of updates to your license.

1. Go to [invictuscad.com/renew](https://invictuscad.com/renew) and enter your license key.
2. Check the new updates-until date, then pay.
3. InvictusCAD picks up the new date the next time it checks in. To get it right away, choose
   **Help > License > Refresh**.

**Renewing early costs you nothing.** The 12 months are added to the end of your current period.
If your updates have already ended, the new 12 months start the day you renew.

Renewing is optional: without it, your license keeps working with every version released before
your updates ended.
