# FAQ

**Does InvictusCAD need the internet?**
Only to start the trial and to activate. After that it works offline; when it's online it checks
in once a day in the background.

**Is there a subscription?**
No. You buy a license once. Renewing updates is optional, or buy the lifetime license and never
renew. See [How licensing works](../license/how-licensing-works.md).

**What happens when my trial or updates end?**
InvictusCAD goes [read-only](../license/read-only.md): you can still open, view, measure and
export your files, but not change them.

**Can I use it on my laptop and my shop PC?**
Yes, on 2 computers at a time. See [Move to another computer](../license/move-computers.md).

**Can I use it for commercial work?**
Yes.

**Does InvictusCAD upload my designs?**
No. Your files stay on your computer.

**What file formats can it read and write?**
Projects are `.ivc` files. Sketches import SVG, DXF, Illustrator and PDF drawings. It exports
sketches and flat parts as DXF and SVG, nests as DXF or SVG, and CAM programs as G-code for
PathPilot, LinuxCNC and GRBL. See [Import and export drawings](../sketching/import-export.md),
[Flat parts](../making/flat-parts.md) and [CAM](../cam/index.md).

**I lost work when InvictusCAD closed. Can I get it back?**
Probably: restart InvictusCAD and choose **Restore**. Earlier saved versions are in
**☰ › File › Show Backups Folder**. See [Files, autosave and backups](../getting-started/files.md).

**Does it run on Mac or Linux?**
Not yet; it's Windows only for now.
