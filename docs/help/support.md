# Get Support

Email [support@cahaba3d.com](mailto:support@cahaba3d.com). It helps to include:

- the version of Cahaba Studio you installed;
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- the `.cb3` file, if the problem is with a particular model;
- a picture of the window: **☰ › Help › Capture Window** (++ctrl+f12++) saves one; the Command Log
  shows where;
- if Cahaba Studio crashed, the newest file in **☰ › Help › Open Crash Reports Folder**.

**Billing, receipts and refunds** are handled by Paddle, our reseller: use the link in your receipt
email or [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

**Lost license key?** Get it emailed again from [cahaba3d.com/license](https://cahaba3d.com/license).
