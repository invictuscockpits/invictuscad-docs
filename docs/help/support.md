# Get Support

Email [support@invictuscad.com](mailto:support@invictuscad.com). It helps to include:

- Your InvictusCAD version (**Help > About**)
- What you did, what you expected, and what happened instead
- The `.ivc` file, if the problem is with a particular model

**Billing, receipts and refunds** are handled by Paddle, our reseller: use the link in your receipt
email or [paddle.net](https://paddle.net).

**Lost license key?** Get it emailed again from [invictuscad.com/license](https://invictuscad.com/license).
