# Free Trial and Activation

## Start the free trial

Choose **Start free trial** when InvictusCAD first opens. You get 30 days with every feature,
no card and no account. The trial belongs to that computer; reinstalling doesn't restart it.

Everything you make during the trial keeps working after you buy.

## Buy a license

Buy at [invictuscad.com/pricing](https://invictuscad.com/pricing). Your license key arrives by
email within a minute or so. It looks like `ABCDE-FGHJK-MNPQR-STVWX`.

## Activate a license

1. In InvictusCAD, choose **Help > License > Enter license key** (or **Enter license key** on the
   start screen).
2. Paste or type the key. Dashes, spaces and lowercase are fine.
3. InvictusCAD activates over the internet and shows your updates date.

One license can be active on **2 computers** at a time. If both slots are taken, InvictusCAD shows
the computers using it and lets you release one. See
[Move to another computer](../license/move-computers.md).

## Working offline

After activating, InvictusCAD runs without an internet connection. About once a month it checks
in quietly to pick up renewals. If it can't reach the server, it simply tries again later.

!!! tip "Lost your key?"
    Get it emailed again from [invictuscad.com/license](https://invictuscad.com/license).
