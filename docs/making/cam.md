# CAM

!!! note "In development"
    The CAM workspace isn't released yet. This page describes what's planned.

The CAM workspace will turn models into G-code for your own machines:

- **Machines you define**: mills up to 4 axes and lathes, with their limits and units
- **Tool libraries** with cutting presets per material
- **Strategies** including facing (with spiral facing), adaptive pockets, contours and drilling
- **Editable post processors**, so the output matches your controller

Until then, use [flat parts and nesting](flat-parts-and-nesting.md) to send 2D work to your
existing CAM program.
