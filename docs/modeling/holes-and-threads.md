# Holes and Threads

## Holes

Place holes on sketch points or by clicking a face.

- Simple, counterbored or countersunk
- Blind (to a depth) or through
- Drill point angle
- Standard sizes fill in tap drills, clearance holes (ISO 273), counterbores and countersinks

## Threads

Threads go in holes or on cylinders, either **cosmetic** (fast; shown as a texture) or **modeled**
(true helical geometry).

| Standard | Range |
|---|---|
| ISO metric coarse and fine | M1 to M64 |
| UNC | #1 to 2" |
| UNF | #0 to 1-1/2" |

Pipe threads (NPT, BSP) and thread classes are planned.
